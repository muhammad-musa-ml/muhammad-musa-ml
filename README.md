I'm finishing my M.S. in Computer Sciences at UW–Madison this December. Most of my research is on LLM security, and prompt injection in particular: what a model with retrieval or tools ends up doing after it reads text an attacker wrote. I'm also working with Prof. Somesh Jha on agentic knowledge-graph extraction from energy-regulatory rate cases.

Outside research I'm the lead software engineer at WUMI Health. I also TA the graduate Introduction to Big Data Systems course, where I look after the programming projects and their autograders, and before this semester I was head TA for Data Science Programming II.

## Research

Right now that's the knowledge-graph extraction work with Prof. Jha.

Last spring a partner and I spent the semester on [indirect prompt injection in RAG](https://github.com/muhammad-musa-ml/IPI-763), and I built most of the attacks and defenses. One mitigation that OWASP recommends backfired by 26 points in our setup.

I've also compared protein language model embeddings as GraphSAGE features for protein-interaction link prediction (the structure-aware SaProt model came out ahead, at AUPRC 0.838), and co-built the first labeled topic-classification dataset for Punjabi in Shahmukhi script.

Papers: [Towards Reproducible Ransomware Analysis](https://doi.org/10.1145/3607505.3607510), ACM CSET 2023, co-author with SRI International. [Extremism on Social Media: Lynching of Priyantha Kumara Diyawadana](https://doi.org/10.1109/ASONAM55673.2022.10068622), IEEE/ACM ASONAM 2022, first author.

## WUMI Health

A health record for Pakistan that the patient owns, written in Flutter on Supabase. Consent is enforced by row-level security in Postgres, so reads across patients and providers have to clear the patient's consent at the database. Seven of the thirteen planned phases are done. Seven clinics, two hospitals and three labs are onboard so far, and I run engineering with three interns.

## How I build

I build with Claude Code and have spent a lot of time on the setup around it. I wrote the methodology skills it loads, and files my agents read go through a prompt-injection scanner, which is my research showing up in my tooling. The [setup notes](./MY-CLAUDE-SETUP.md) go into it.
