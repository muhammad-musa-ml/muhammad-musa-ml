# Logo tiles

The logos in this folder come from two projects:

- [skill-icons](https://github.com/tandpfun/skill-icons), MIT license (full text below). Files are used as distributed, in both themes.
- [Simple Icons](https://simpleicons.org) v16.32.0, CC0 1.0. These glyphs are drawn on tiles that copy skill-icons' shape and colours, and a few brand colours are lightened or darkened so they stay readable on those tiles.

Every logo is a trademark of its owner and appears here only to name a tool.
